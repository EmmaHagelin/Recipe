Pot-au-feu

It's one of the classic French comfort foods: beef slowly simmered until tender with root vegetables, leeks and herbs in a deeply savoury broth.

  • Time: 3 - 3½ hours
  • Portioner: 4

Ingredients

  • 800 grams - 1 kilogram of fransyska
  • 2 litre cold water, or enough to cover the meat
  • 2 beef stock cubes
  • 1 large onion
  • 2 garlic cloves, or to your hearts content
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme, or fresh sticks of thyme
  • 8-10 whole black peppercorns
  • Salt to taste
  • 4 carrots
  • 1 small leek
  • 500-600 grams of potatoes
  • 2 parsnips
  • Fresh parsley

How to

  1. Start the broth: Place the whole piece of beef in a large pot and cover with cold water. Slowly bring it to a gentle simmer. Skim off the foam that rises to the surface during the first 15–20 minutes. This helps keep the broth clear.
  2. Add the aromatics: Add the stock cubes, onion (halved), garlic, bay leaves, thyme and peppercorns. Keep the liquid at a very gentle simmer, with only occasional bubbles. Cover partially and cook for about 1½–2 hours.
  3. Add the vegetables: Peel the carrots, parsnips and potatoes. Cut the carrots and parsnips into large pieces. Wash the leek thoroughly and cut it into thick sections. Add the carrots, parsnips and celeriac first. After about 15 minutes, add the leeks and potatoes.
  4. Simmer until tender: Continue cooking for another 30–45 minutes, or longer if necessary, until the vegetables are tender and the beef is easily pierced with a fork. If the vegetables finish before the beef, remove them and keep them warm. The meat is ready when tender, not just when the cooking time is up.
  5. Serve: Taste and adjust the salt. Remove the bay leaves and peppercorns, then slice the beef across the grain. Serve the beef and vegetables in shallow bowls with plenty of hot broth, fresh parsley and Dijon mustard on the side.
